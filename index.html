SWITCH — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026
App: SWITCH
Developer: ODDORBIT GAMES

1. Introduction

ODDORBIT GAMES (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the SWITCH mobile game handles information when you use the application. SWITCH is an offline reaction game designed for entertainment.

2. Information We Collect

SWITCH does not collect, request, or transmit personal information to servers operated by ODDORBIT GAMES. The game does not require account registration or login, and does not request your name, email address, phone number, or location.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

SWITCH stores limited game preferences locally on your device, including your best score and sound preference. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to ODDORBIT GAMES. The app does not use an online leaderboard.

4. Advertising and Analytics

This version of SWITCH does not contain advertisements and does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking technologies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. SWITCH operates offline and does not send gameplay information to external servers.

6. Data Security

Game preferences and best scores are stored locally. Their security depends in part on the security of your device. ODDORBIT GAMES does not maintain a remote database of player information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored scores and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app’s stored data or uninstall the application. We do not retain personal information on our servers.

8. Children’s Privacy

SWITCH does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. No personal information is required to play the game. The app’s intended audience and any applicable child-directed requirements are described separately in its Google Play declarations.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app or its privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this webpage with a revised effective date.
